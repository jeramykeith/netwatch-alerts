HomeLab NetWatch Alerts – Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026 · Program operated by HomeLab (sole proprietor)

Who we are

HomeLab (sole proprietor) operates NetWatch, home-network monitoring and parental-control software, and HomeLab NetWatch Alerts, the program that sends NetWatch's alerts by text message (SMS). This Privacy Policy covers the text-message program.

What information we collect

  • The mobile phone number the account owner enters in the NetWatch dashboard, and whether text alerts are switched on.
  • The alert messages sent to that number, such as a request for more internet time, a daily time limit being reached, or a blocked website.
  • Replies to those messages, such as YES, YES 30, NO, STOP or HELP.

How we use it

The phone number is used only to send HomeLab NetWatch Alerts to the account owner who opted in. Replies are used only to approve or decline a request for more internet time and to honor opt-out and help requests. The information is not used for advertising or marketing.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in information are never sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Messages pass through Twilio, our SMS provider, and mobile carriers only so that they can be delivered.

Where it is kept

Settings and message history are stored on the NetWatch server on the home network it monitors. They are not copied anywhere else, except to the SMS provider for delivery. Removing the number in the dashboard deletes it from the settings.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with activity on the home network. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will get one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to turn texts back on. Reply HELP for help.
