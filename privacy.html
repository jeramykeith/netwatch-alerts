NetWatch Alerts – Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 · Program operated by HomeLab, a household (sole proprietor)

What this program is

NetWatch is a private home-network monitor for one household. NetWatch Alerts sends text messages (SMS) about that household's own home network to the household's account owner only. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign up.

What information is collected

  • One mobile phone number: the account owner's own number, which the owner enters on the private NetWatch settings page.
  • The alert messages sent to that number, such as a child asking for more internet time, a daily time limit being reached, or a blocked website.
  • The owner's replies to those messages, such as YES, YES 30 or NO.

How the information is used

The phone number is used only to send these alerts to the account owner. Replies are used only to approve or decline a request for more internet time. The information is not used for advertising, marketing or any other purpose.

Sharing

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in information are never sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Messages are delivered through Twilio, the SMS provider, only so that they can reach the phone. Nothing else is shared with anyone.

Where it is kept

Settings and message history stay on the household's own computer at home. They are not copied anywhere else except to the SMS provider for delivery.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with what happens on the home network, usually a few messages a day. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will get one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to turn texts back on. Reply HELP for help.
